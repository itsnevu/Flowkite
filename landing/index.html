Install

Ready in two minutes.

  1. 1 Add to Chrome

    One click from the Chrome Web Store.

    Add to Chrome

  2. 2 Add your AI key

    Open Settings in the side panel and paste a key.

  3. 3 Ask

    Type what you need and approve the plan.

Install from the zip instead

  1. Download flowkite.zip and unzip it.

  2. Open this address:

    chrome://extensions/

  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).

  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it free?

Yes. No paid tier and no account. You only pay your AI provider for what you use, and nothing at all with a local model through Ollama.

Where do my API keys go?

They stay in your browser and go straight to your AI provider. There is no Flowkite server.

What data leaves my machine?

Only what a task needs: the page it is working on, the titles of your open tabs, and screenshots when vision is on, sent to the AI provider you chose. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work with local models?

Yes, through Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and Edge.
